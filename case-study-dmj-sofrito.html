DMJ Sofrito | Sensory Culinary Cinema

Capturing the kinetic sizzle, raw botanical textures, and artisanal heritage of DMJ Sofrito to drive e-commerce engagement and retail supermarket expansion across Westchester and New York.

Client & Principal Dulce Jorge, Founder & CEO DMJ Sofrito / Fresco Sofrito by DMJ
Location 55 Webster Ave, Suite 410 New Rochelle, NY
Optical & Sound Format High-Speed 4K Cinema / Macro 32-Bit Float Sensory Foley
Primary Objective Retail Buyer Syndication High-Conversion D2C Video Ads

“Northzero elevated our handcrafted seasoning process into visual art. The macro camera work and lighting captured the genuine freshness and culture behind DMJ Sofrito in a way that resonated immediately with our customers and retail partners.”

Dulce Jorge
Founder & CEO, DMJ Sofrito LLC · New Rochelle, NY
Phase 01

The Sensory Challenge

Artisanal culinary condiments cannot rely on conventional pack-shot photography. DMJ Sofrito is built on small-batch craftsmanship: hand-chopped peppers, freshly harvested culantro, garlic, and slow-simmered aromatics. The primary production challenge was translating fragrance, botanical freshness, and kitchen warmth through digital glass.

The Objective: Create a sensory brand film that proves the uncompromising freshness of raw ingredients, demonstrates home culinary versatility, and establishes the brand as an authentic authority in regional specialty grocers and supermarket buyer presentations.

Phase 02

Macro Optics & Kinetic Lighting

Northzero engineered a dedicated tabletop culinary lighting stage utilizing high-CRI cool LED key lights to preserve the natural crispness of raw herbs under intense illumination without heat wilting the produce. Specialized 100mm macro and probe optics captured micro-droplets of olive oil, steam convection, and the granular density of the blended seasoning paste as it engaged a hot pan.

Capturing high-frame-rate passes at 120fps enabled us to decelerate rapid culinary actions—the acoustic sizzle of aromatics, wooden spoon swirls, and the physical pouring motion. Paired with 32-bit float audio capturing raw knife impacts and mortar-and-pestle foley, the film provides a visceral sensory rhythm that standard commercial photography cannot match.

Phase 03

Deliverables & Distribution

Modern food and beverage distribution requires assets optimized across direct-to-consumer e-commerce funnels, wholesale buyer pitch decks, and high-retention social environments.

  • 1x 4K Master Brand Anthem (Full narrative vignette with founder presentation)
  • 4x High-Speed Micro-Cutdowns (15s & 30s clips formatted for paid social conversion)
  • Looping Web Heroes (Silent, optimized horizontal videos for website landing pages)
  • Print-Ready Macro Stills (High-contrast product stills for packaging & wholesale sheets)
  • Master ProRes 422 HQ Archive (Long-term master media repository delivered to client)
