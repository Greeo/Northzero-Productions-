DMJ Sofrito | Tactile Culinary Cinema

Capturing the kinetic sizzle, raw botanical textures, and artisanal heritage of DMJ Sofrito to drive e-commerce engagement and retail supermarket expansion.

Client & Brand DMJ Sofrito LLC
Production Format High-Speed 4K Cinema / Macro Optics
Primary Objective Digital Conversion & Retail Syndication
Delivery Timeline 3-Day Turnaround from Wrap
Phase 01

The Sensory Challenge

Artisanal culinary condiments cannot rely on conventional pack-shot photography. DMJ Sofrito is built on hand-chopped peppers, fresh culantro, garlic, and slow-cooked aromatics. The primary challenge was communicating fragrance, warmth, and visceral kitchen craft through digital screens.

The Objective: Create a sensory brand vignette that highlights the raw freshness of the ingredients, demonstrates effortless home culinary application, and establishes the brand as an authentic authority in regional specialty markets.

Phase 02

Macro Craft & Lighting

Northzero built a specialized tabletop kitchen lighting stage using directional key fixtures to mimic natural golden-hour window light. We utilized specialized macro cinema glass to capture micro-droplets of olive oil, steam convection, and the rich granular texture of the puréed base as it hit the pan.

Capturing high frame rate motion (120fps) allowed us to slow down volatile culinary moments—the sizzle of aromatics, spoon swirls, and the physical pouring motion—giving the final film a tactile, sensory rhythm that photography cannot replicate.

Phase 03

Deliverables & Distribution

Modern food and beverage distribution requires assets optimized across direct-to-consumer e-commerce, supermarket buyer presentations, and mobile social channels.

  • 1x 4K Master Brand Anthem (Full narrative vignette with founder presentation)
  • 4x High-Speed Micro-Cutdowns (15s & 30s clips formatted for paid social conversion)
  • Looping Web Heroes (Silent, optimized horizontal videos for website landing pages)
  • Print-Ready Macro Stills (High-contrast product stills for packaging & wholesale sheets)
  • Master ProRes 422 HQ Archive (Long-term master media repository delivered to client)
