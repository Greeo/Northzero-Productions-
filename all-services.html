Integrated Operating Model

From Strategy Through Final Delivery

Rather than operating as a fragmented vendor, Northzero provides an integrated production continuum. Every stage is led by principal direction to safeguard brand integrity, regulatory compliance, and cinematic caliber.

01 / STRATEGY

Pre-Production & Scoping

Creative architecture, distribution mapping, historical research, location surveys, sightline analysis, shooting scripts, and strict permitting coordination.

02 / PRODUCTION

Principal Cinematography

On-location and controlled studio execution, FAA Part 107 commercial sUAS flight operations, large-format cinema sensors, and calibrated optical systems.

03 / POST-PRODUCTION

Finishing & Mastery

Color-managed DaVinci Resolve grading, precision sound design and acoustic scoring, visual fidelity mastering, subtitle formatting, and Apple ProRes preservation exports.

04 / DISTRIBUTION

Cinematic Web Environments

Bespoke campaign landing environments, digital screening portals, and high-performance interactive architectures engineered to host our cinematic visual assets.

Archival Mastery

Deliverables packaged in Apple ProRes 422 HQ alongside optimized 4K web profiles for generational fidelity and permanent preservation.

Airspace Compliance

Full commercial FAA Part 107 authorization with LAANC flight clearances, risk mitigation protocols, and regional airspace management.

Color Science

Strict Academy Color Encoding System (ACES) or color-managed pipelines ensuring identical tonal and material rendering across all panels.

Contract Discretion

Rigorous non-disclosure protocols for unreleased architectural developments, off-market real estate, and confidential civic hearings.
