Phase 01

The Spatial Challenge

Multi-million dollar architectural estates cannot be accurately communicated through static wide-angle photography alone. The residence featured vast floor-to-ceiling curtain walls with extreme dynamic range variations between morning daylight and evening river fog.

The Objective: Capture the authentic spatial flow from the cantilevered exterior terraces into the vaulted interior living spaces without artificial, over-lit real estate glare. The prospective acquisition syndicates required a film that captured emotional presence, architectural integrity, and the elevation of the property above the Hudson River.

Phase 02

Founder-Led Execution

Northzero executed a two-day principal photography schedule synchronized to the solar path. Using calibrated cinema optics, our team timed interior tracking sequences to coincide with natural architectural cove illumination—preserving the architect's intended shadow lines and texture.

Under FAA Part 107 regulatory oversight, twilight aerial sweeps were captured at exact elevations to emphasize private parcel boundaries and direct river proximity without disrupting municipal noise standards.

Phase 03

Deliverables & Syndication

A premium listing requires multiple tiers of distribution tailored for buyer presentations, high-net-worth social acquisition, and MLS compliance.

  • 4K Master Acquisition Film (3:00 horizontal master with custom acoustic score)
  • Unbranded MLS Presentation Cut (Strict adherence to MLS non-promotional regulations)
  • 3x 9:16 Mobile-First Vertical Cuts (Optimized for private buyer syndication & Instagram)
  • Archival High-Resolution Still Set (Color-graded frame grabs for editorial print marketing)
  • ProRes 422 HQ Masters (Long-term studio archives delivered via secure cloud portal)
